CYNTHION

An isometric party-based CRPG set on Earth's Moon, ~300 years after colonization.

Mars took the future. The lunar colony is only just realising it's been quietly written off.

Lead a party through what's left. Explore the cracked domes, hear out the survivors, fight where you have to. Help decide what Cynthion becomes.
