Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how cynthiongame.com ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you visit this website or submit your email address through our signup form.

Information We Collect. When you submit our mailing-list signup form, we collect the email address you provide. We do not collect any other personal information about you on this website. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts.

How We Use Your Information. We use your email address to send you occasional updates about Cynthion, including development progress and the launch announcement. We do not use your information for any other purpose.

How We Share Your Information. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share your email address only with the third-party service providers listed below, and only to the extent necessary to operate the mailing list.

Third-Party Service Providers. Our signup form is processed by Web3Forms, which delivers your submission to our email inbox hosted by Google. Your submission is also accessible through our domain registrar's email forwarding service. Typography on this site is loaded from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when the page loads. Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how it handles your information.

Data Retention. We retain your email address until you ask us to remove it, or until the project concludes and the mailing list is closed, whichever occurs first.

Your Rights. You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. You also have the right to withdraw consent and unsubscribe from our mailing list at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

International Users. If you are accessing this website from outside Australia, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in Australia or other countries where our service providers operate.

Children's Privacy. This website is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security. We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will indicate any changes by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact. For any questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your rights, contact info@cynthiongame.com.
