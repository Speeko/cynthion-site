Privacy

This page explains what happens when you submit your email address through the form on this site.

What's collected. Only the email address you type into the form. No name, no location, no cookies, no analytics.

Where it goes. The form posts your address to Web3Forms (a form-to-email processor) which forwards the submission as an email to the developer's personal Gmail account. Web3Forms acts as a passthrough; we don't keep your address on their servers.

What it's used for. Occasional development updates and the launch announcement for Cynthion. Nothing else. The mailing list is not shared, sold, or rented to anyone.

How long it's kept. Until the launch announcement is sent, or until you ask to be removed — whichever comes first.

Removing your email. Email info@cynthiongame.com with the subject "remove" and the address you signed up with. You'll be removed within a few days.

Third-party policies. Web3Forms has its own privacy policy. Google Fonts (used to load the typography on this page) sees your IP when the page loads.

Contact. Questions about any of this go to info@cynthiongame.com.
